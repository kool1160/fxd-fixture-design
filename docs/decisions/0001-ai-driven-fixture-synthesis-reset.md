# Decision 0001 — AI-Driven Fixture Synthesis and Project-Control Reset

- **Status:** Accepted by product owner; implementation pending governance PR review
- **Date:** 2026-08-09
- **Authority:** Chris Hilton
- **Issue:** #66
- **Supersedes:** M32 / Issue #57 / PR #54 product path; conflicting portions of Issues #59 and #63

## Decision

FXD will use live AI to author the fixture strategy that drives real geometry creation. Deterministic systems will compile, author, validate, reject, and report evidence. Qualified human engineering judgment will decide fixture practicality and production authority.

The prior architecture—deterministically generate a fixture, then attach an advisory AI proposal—is rejected as the continuing product path.

Development control will use a LaserX-style Review-Control ↔ Codex loop rather than an autonomous multi-agent Foreman that selects and reviews its own work.

## Evidence behind the decision

- FXD has a real OpenAI provider adapter, but normal launch does not prove it is configured or used.
- The provider path may fall back to a deterministic proposal when unavailable or failed.
- M32 acceptance/self-check paths intentionally run offline.
- M32 fixture geometry is generated by deterministic code before being bound to proposal evidence.
- Real fixture examples were reduced to abstract knowledge records rather than executable feature-response precedents.
- Repeated qualified human reviews rejected fixture practicality despite extensive passing software evidence.

## Consequences

- Issue #57 and PR #54 are closed as Superseded, not Complete.
- Their branch and evidence remain available for selective salvage.
- Product implementation is held during the Issue #66 governance reset.
- A new M33 AI-Driven Fixture Synthesis Proof may be activated after governance acceptance.
- AI Design and deterministic/offline mode must be visibly separate; no silent fallback.
- Product reconstruction and structured private precedents become first-class requirements.
- At most one bounded AI repair cycle is permitted in the first proof.
- The first proof succeeds only after qualified human acceptance that the fixture is materially buildable and usable.

## Protected boundaries preserved

- immutable source CAD;
- CAD-neutral architecture;
- deterministic engineering truth;
- restricted typed commands;
- local-first privacy;
- dependency/provider licensing and cost controls;
- no customer/employer CAD in public automation;
- no automatic production approval or release;
- qualified human engineering approval.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit only after the M33 proof produces measured evidence that a different authority split is necessary or impossible. A provider preference or a passing software demo is not enough.
